If an ad appears, open Not working? in the app. Choose whether it appeared in the video player, below the video, in the home feed or in Shorts. Several locations can be selected. Confirm the setup checklist, then review the report before choosing Email report or Share report. A comment is optional. Do not include account passwords, private keys or browsing traffic.

If filtering does not start, run the connection checks. Confirm the local certificate is installed and trusted, and that the filter is active. Fully close and reopen YouTube after enabling the filter. Another VPN can interfere with the connection.

If playback stops working, stop the filter and retry playback. Report the problem with the checklist and optional diagnostics. The app cannot guarantee that every advertising format will be removed.

To remove the setup, use Remove connection in the app and remove LocalFilter Personal CA in Settings > General > VPN & Device Management. Uninstalling the app alone may leave the certificate profile or Keychain entry on the device.

No support report is uploaded automatically. The app prepares an email with an attachment; you decide whether to send it.
