ADBLOCK YOUTUBEAPP

On-device ad filtering

Adblock YoutubeApp filters recognized advertising metadata used by the official YouTube app on iPhone and iPad. It requires manual setup of a local certificate and VPN configuration.

The first release is being prepared for App Store review and is not yet available to download. Some ads may still appear. The app does not filter Safari or remove sponsorships embedded in videos.

Read our privacy policy to understand local HTTPS processing, or visit support for setup and troubleshooting help.
