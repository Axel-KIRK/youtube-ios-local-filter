Privacy policy

The filter decrypts selected YouTube HTTPS traffic on this device to remove advertising metadata. This traffic can include account headers and video requests. Requests are forwarded to YouTube; they are not sent to a server operated by the app developer.

The app does not include analytics or advertising SDKs, sell data or track you across apps. No ongoing analytics are sent. YouTube continues to process the requests it receives under its own privacy policy.

The certificate private key stays in this device’s Keychain and does not sync. Setup preferences, progress and an aggregate filtering total are stored locally in UserDefaults and may be included in device backups. Traffic is processed in memory rather than saved as browsing history.

Operational logs contain event names, error categories and counters, not request bodies, account headers or private keys. Diagnostics you choose to share may reveal connection and setup information.

Diagnostic history is off by default. If you enable it in Not working?, up to 40 predefined steps are kept in protected local cache files excluded from backups. Steps older than 24 hours are discarded on the next launch, foreground check, recording or report. You can delete this history, disable it, or exclude it from a report. These logs contain app setup actions and check results, not screen recordings or YouTube traffic. No analytics or logs are uploaded automatically.

In Not working?, you can prepare and review a report with your checklist answers, optional network/frequency/VPN/ad-location answers and YouTube version, optional comment, app and iOS versions, connection state, technical checks, error categories and recent app actions. The app does not automatically attach videos, URLs, account headers, passwords, certificates or private keys.

Email report prepares a message to mackirk.dev@gmail.com with a JSON attachment. You choose whether to send it. Your sender name, email address, message and report will be visible to the developer and handled by your email provider. Share report passes the report to the app you select. PostHog and Sentry receive nothing from this feature.

The temporary attachment is removed when you close the report or create a new one. Copies saved or sent through other apps remain under those apps’ controls. To request deletion of a received report, email the developer with the report reference. Reports are used for troubleshooting and are not sold or used for advertising.

Stop turns filtering off. Remove connection removes the VPN configuration. To remove the trusted certificate, open Settings → General → VPN & Device Management and remove LocalFilter Personal CA. Uninstalling the app does not necessarily remove its Keychain entry or the certificate profile.

Replace certificate generates a new local key and certificate. The old profile must be removed in Settings, and the new certificate installed and trusted before filtering can resume.

Do not post account headers, private keys or browsing traffic in public reports.
